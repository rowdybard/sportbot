## Ongoing sourcing for this niche
- **FB Marketplace saved searches** (40 mi, newest first): `nintendo lot`, `old video games`, `n64`, `gamecube`, `ps2 lot`, `game boy`, `untested`, `for parts`, `grandpa stereo`, `receiver vintage`, `pioneer receiver`, `marantz`, `technics`, `subwoofer box`, `amp kicker`, `rockford`, `studio gear`, `3d printer`.
- **Price check every console or game** on PriceCharting (loose / CIB). Buy game lots at **40% or less of PriceCharting loose value**.
- **Car audio is seasonal:** buy Nov–Feb when people clear garages and cars, and sell Mar–Jun when install season starts. Lansing inventory is thin right now, so set alerts and wait.
- **MSU Surplus Store** (Fridays 8–3:30 in store): laptops, iPads and audio gear. Only buy below eBay-sold minus 30%.
- **Estate sales, last day after 1pm:** stereo consoles, receivers and old game systems get missed by pickers.
