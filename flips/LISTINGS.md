## Delta Township haul (sort into lots)
Price each piece using eBay **sold** comps. Suggested approach:
- **Bikes:** list each separately ($40–150 depending on brand; Trek/Specialized/Giant higher). Air the tires, wipe the chain.
- **Drone:** check the model on eBay sold listings. Even dead ones sell for parts ($20–60).
- **Antique banknotes:** don't sell locally. Check eBay sold listings or take them to a coin shop for an offer. Could be the best item in the pile.
- **Power/garden tools (trimmers, hedge trimmers, vacuums):** list as a "garage tools lot" for $60–120, or split out the brand names.
- **Leftovers:** one "garage sale box" listing at $20, or post free to clear space.
