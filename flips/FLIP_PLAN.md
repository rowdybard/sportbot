## Snowblower flip playbook
1. **Before you pay:** check the auger turns, the tires hold air, and there's no metal grinding. "Needs belts" is cheap; "needs a transmission" is not.
2. **Parts:** find the model/serial plate, then order OEM belts on Amazon/eBay (~$20–30 each). Ariens belt covers show up on eBay for ~$20–30.
3. **Service:** drain the old gas, add fresh gas plus stabilizer, put in a new spark plug ($5), change the oil (10W-30 or 5W-30 small engine), and grease the fittings.
4. **Photos:** take them outside, clean, from 3 angles plus the auger. Shoot a **20-second video of it starting and throwing gravel or leaves**. This alone lets you ask 20–30% more.
5. **List ~Nov 1**, then **re-post the day the first snow is forecast.** Lansing usually gets its first real snow mid-to-late November. Demand spikes the next morning.
6. **Price:** list about 15% above your floor and don't budge in the first 48 hours after snowfall.
   - Floor for the Ariens ST824: $175. Floor for the JD: $200.
