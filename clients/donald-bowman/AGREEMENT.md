Plain-language terms, written to be read aloud by a screen reader.

Month to month. Cancel any time by phone or email.

## Changes: $25 each

Batch discount when sent together in one request:
- 1 change: $25
- 3 changes: $60
- 5 changes: $90
Bigger work (a new page, a new service with booking changes) gets a price agreed first. Nothing is charged without Don saying yes.
Don can request changes however is easiest for him: phone call, text, voice message or email. Daniel reads each change back to him before it goes live, and confirms once it's done.
